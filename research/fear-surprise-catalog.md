# Fear and Surprise Candidate Catalog

**Status:** research candidates only. **Checked:** 2026-08-02.

This ledger supplies 300 explicitly countable placements: 10 canonical
resources in each category for each family, placed once at each of the three
intensity nodes. A placement is an editorial possibility, never a claim that a
work changes, treats, or reliably regulates anyone's emotion. All entries
remain `candidate` until a maintainer checks the actual edition/release/page,
availability, rights, warnings, and route fit.

## Reading the tables

- Each numbered row is one canonical resource and produces **three**
  placements, one in every intensity column. Thus every table is `10 x 3 =
  30` candidate placements and every mood/category cell has exactly 10.
- `A`, `F`, and `T` mean Apprehension, Fear, and Terror. `D`, `S`, and `M`
  mean Distraction, Surprise, and Amazement. The short text in each cell is
  the placement-specific rationale, not a general endorsement.
- Sources are stable, public catalog/platform search URLs. `OL` is Open
  Library, `AM` Apple Music, `TMDB` The Movie Database, `NPR` NPR's official
  search, `GG` Greater Good's official search, and `AP` Apple Podcasts. They
  establish a recheck path; they are not quality evidence.
- Warnings: `horror`, `violence`, `death`, `threat`, `disturbing imagery`,
  `body horror`, and `war` are potential content warnings to confirm before
  publication. Omitted warnings mean **unknown**, not none.

candidates are primarily suspense, horror, or fear-literacy works. Do not
surface them by default for a person who signals acute distress; offer a clear
return to calmer routes and disclose warnings before opening a resource.
