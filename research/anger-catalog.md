# Anger Candidate Catalog

**Status:** candidate research only  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** 150 explicit placements: 10 canonical candidates in each of five
categories, placed across Annoyance, Anger, and Rage. These are editorial
possibilities, not clinical guidance and not claims that a work will change a
person's emotions.

## Counting and verification rules

Each resource row below counts as three placements because the final three
columns give a distinct intensity-specific rationale. All source links are
stable catalog, publisher, or platform searches that must be resolved to a
direct canonical page before publication. `Candidate` means title and creator
still need a maintainer's availability, warnings, rights, access, and route
review. `CW` is a prompt for closer review, not a complete content rating.
