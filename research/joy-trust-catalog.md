# Joy and Trust Candidate Catalog

**Status:** research candidates only. **Checked:** 2026-08-02. This file is
an intake ledger, not a published recommendation list. A placement is an
editorial possibility, not a statement that a work will improve, treat, or
reliably change anyone's mood.

## Scope and counting

This catalog covers the Joy family (`serenity`, `joy`, `ecstasy`) and the
Trust family (`acceptance`, `trust`, `admiration`) across books, music, movies,
blogs/articles, and podcasts. Each family/category table has ten canonical works.
Every row contains **three explicit placement records**, one per intensity.
That yields `2 families x 5 categories x 10 works x 3 moods = 300 candidate
placements`.

All source links are Open Library title/creator searches, TMDB title searches,
or publisher/platform lookup pages. Links are discovery and metadata paths;
they are not evidence that an item is safe or available everywhere. Before a
placement can become `verified`, a maintainer must open the primary page,
confirm the title/creator/edition, check rights and region availability,
review warnings, and replace the lookup with a stable canonical item URL.

Unless a row says otherwise: language is `en`; availability is `mixed`;
content-warning status is `pending review`; and review status is `candidate`.
Book time means
reading commitment; music means one track; movie time means one feature; an
article or podcast has its stated approximate duration. Candidate rationales
are intentionally non-clinical and describe format, tone, or use context only.
