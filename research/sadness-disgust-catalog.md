# Sadness and Disgust Candidate Catalog

**Status:** research candidates only  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** 300 explicitly countable recommendation placements: 10 canonical
resources in each of five categories for each family, placed once in each of
the family's three intensity nodes. This is a research ledger, not clinical
guidance or a claim that any work will change a person's emotion.

## How to read this ledger

Every row is one canonical resource and contains exactly three placements,
one for each intensity. The linked source is a stable catalog or official
platform search, used to re-check title and creator before publication.
All rows remain `candidate`: a maintainer still needs to confirm current
availability, rights, content warnings, accessibility, and the exact
canonical destination. `CW` flags themes needing a closer publishing review;
an omitted flag is not a claim that a work is free of difficult material.

The columns headed **low**, **primary**, and **high** contain compact,
resource-specific editorial rationales, not therapeutic promises. They count
as three distinct placements. Reuse is intentionally capped at these three
within a family/category.
