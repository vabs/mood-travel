# Anticipation Candidate Catalog

**Status:** research candidates only. **Checked:** 2026-08-02. These
placements are editorial starting points, not claims about changing mood,
improving health, or predicting an outcome. `Vigilance` must never be framed as
encouragement to remain unsafe, hyper-alert, or fearful.

## Scope and counting

This ledger covers the Anticipation family: `interest` (low intensity),
`anticipation` (primary), and `vigilance` (high intensity), across five
categories. Each category has ten canonical candidate resources. Every row
has three separately written placement rationales, so the explicit count is
`5 categories x 10 resources x 3 moods = 150 candidate placements`.

All resources are `candidate`, language `en`, availability `mixed`, and use
source lookups rather than a publication-approved canonical link. Before any
item is verified or published, open the source page, confirm the exact work
and creator, replace the lookup URL with an item page, check region/rights,
and review the complete work or an authoritative synopsis for warnings. Times
are approximate. Empty warning cells mean **not yet reviewed**, not no warning.
